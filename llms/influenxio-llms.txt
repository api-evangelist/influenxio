# Influenxio

> Influenxio (圈圈) is a Taiwan-based Instagram influencer-marketing platform that connects brands
> with a database of micro-influencers ("KOC") through an AI-powered matching system. Brands search,
> invite, and collaborate with creators at scale for brand awareness, traffic, and affiliate
> marketing, including a pay-per-click affiliate model and content-authorization workflows for
> reusing creator-generated content. It is a B2B SaaS product for marketers, not for developers.

IMPORTANT — no API: Influenxio publishes **no public API, no developer portal, no SDK, no webhooks,
and no machine-readable specification of any kind**. There is nothing here for an agent to call.
The host `api.influenxio.com` is the web application's private backend; it returns HTTP 401
`{"error":{"code":401,"message":"Unauthorized"}}` on every path, including `/openapi.json`, `/docs`,
`/graphql`, `/mcp` and every `/.well-known/*` path. Do not attempt to integrate with it.
This file is generated by API Evangelist from the public catalog record, not published by Influenxio.

## Pricing

- [Plans](https://www.influenxio.com/en-US/plans): two published tiers.
  - 進階彈性方案 (Advanced Flexible Plan) — self-service; no listed subscription price. Collaboration
    fees are quoted per creator and offset in full against a prepaid wallet (儲值金). Unlimited
    collaborations, no region or industry restriction. Entry is free registration.
  - 企業客製方案 (Enterprise Custom Plan) — managed service from NT$200,000. Free proposal and pitch,
    creator communication, post review, analytics and optimization.
- 30-day satisfaction guarantee; credit card online, bank transfer on request.
- No API plan, developer tier, or usage-based API pricing is published.
